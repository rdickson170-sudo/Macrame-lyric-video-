Privacy Policy

Lyric Video Studio, designed by MACRAME. Effective 24 October 2026.

What the app does

Lyric Video Studio lets you turn a song and lyrics you provide into a lyric video. All the work happens on your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your files

The songs, lyrics, artwork, photos and videos you use stay on your device. They are not uploaded to any server by this app. Style settings and saved styles are kept in your device's local browser storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google may receive your IP address and device information as part of serving those fonts, under Google's Privacy Policy. After the first visit the fonts may be cached on your device.

Your content

You are responsible for using only music, lyrics and images that you own or have permission to use.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
